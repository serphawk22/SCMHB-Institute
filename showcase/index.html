End-to-End Operational Lifecycle

Student Journey Workflow

A structured six-step workflow guiding prospective students from initial inquiry to enrolled student status.

01
Step 1

Lead Captured

Student inquiry captured automatically from website forms, helpline calls, or campus visits.

02
Step 2

Lead Assigned

System allocates candidate to an admissions counselor based on workload and program domain.

03
Step 3

Counseling

Counselor logs career goals, program details, curriculum alignment, and campus visit details.

04
Step 4

Follow-up

Scheduled follow-up reminders prompt timely calls, document submissions, or demo sessions.

05
Step 5

Enrollment

Application approved, course seat confirmed, batch assigned, and student profile provisioned.

06
Step 6

Fee Tracking

Payment schedule setup, initial fee recorded, digital receipt issued, and metrics updated.
